## What's new in Aretas 0.1.14

*2026-08-24*

Aretas now works through your whole mailbox on its own, so introductions and contact changes actually reach you.

- The background pass through your mail now keeps its place instead of losing everything it had done whenever a run was interrupted, and it keeps working through mail already on your Mac when an account needs reconnecting.
- An introduction made inside a forwarded message is now recognised as an introduction, not just a message you were copied on.
- When someone tells you they have a new number, a new address, or a new job, that now arrives as a card you can accept — and accepting it writes to your real contact.
- Introductions that arrive by text message are now found too, not just the ones in email.
- Newest mail is looked at first, you choose how much counts as recent, and repeat passes skip work they have already done.
- A status panel tells you what Aretas is doing right now, and says plainly when it is stuck rather than showing a spinner forever.
- After every run you can see exactly which contacts changed, taken from the record of what was actually written.
- Contacts that look like the same person twice are gathered for you to review. Nothing is ever merged without you deciding, and a copy of your data is saved first.
- When someone new shares a name with a contact you already have, Aretas asks instead of guessing: same person, or someone different? Same person adds the new details to that contact; different person adds them separately.
- Adding someone now works on the first press, and their card opens in Contacts so you can check or edit it. Lists has a search box, and scanning no longer hides the introductions already waiting for you.
