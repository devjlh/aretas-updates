## What's new in Aretas 0.1.13

*2026-08-19*

Introductions now surfaces the people you've just met, and Tagging is now Lists — the same word your iPhone uses.

- The Tagging tab is now called Lists, matching what you see on your iPhone under Contacts. A contact you add to a List here reconciles into that same List on your phone, so the two never drift apart.
- Introductions now looks across your most recent conversations in every account each time it runs, so someone you just met by email or text surfaces right away instead of waiting behind your older mail. Cards stay in your queue until you review them — nothing ages out on its own.
- Screens that could briefly freeze while a large list or a big mailbox loaded now stay responsive, and Introductions stays quick even while the rest of your mail is still downloading in the background.
- The overview no longer briefly claims no accounts are connected while it is still loading them.
