## What's new in Aretas 0.1.11

*2026-08-19*

Opening Sources no longer freezes Aretas.

- The Sources screen — where your mail accounts, Messages, and call history live — could lock the whole app up the moment it opened, with a spinning wait cursor and no way forward. It now opens smoothly and stays responsive.
- Your information was never touched by this. It was only the screen struggling to draw itself; nothing was being read, changed, or sent while it was stuck.
